--- title: AI attribution badges layout: page permalink: /badges/ description: A small set of badges for saying how AI was actually used in a piece of work — who led, and how much of the thing the tool brought into being. --- {%- comment -%} The badge picker. Every badge on this page is a real file under /badge/, generated by bin/badge from bin/badge-assets/spec.py; nothing here draws a badge itself. _data/ai_badges.yml is the manifest bin/badge writes, and it is what both this page and _includes/ai-badge.html read. Styling stays on currentColor and opacity rather than fixed colours so it holds up across the theme skins -- the badges bring their own colour and adapt to prefers-color-scheme on their own. {%- endcomment -%}

Six badges, from two questions. The preposition says who led the work: with keeps the colour on the edge, because a person did the thing and used a tool; by floods the badge, because the tool did it. The verb says how much of the thing the tool actually brought into being — edited is quiet, created is loud.

Every tool wears the same spectrum. Only the logo plate carries a brand colour, so the whole family reads as one mark rather than as somebody else's advertising.
